## Section-by-Section Analysis

### 1. Hero Section [Score: 6/10]
**Findings:**
- Brand wordmark is dominant; campaign line is clear.
- Primary CTA was “Open on GitHub” — wrong for App Download intent.
- Lore under the brand is thin; little mystical atmosphere beyond the desk shot.
- No fake trust badges (correct); missing stance proof near CTA.

**Fixes (Priority: HIGH):**
- Make **Download for Windows** the primary CTA; GitHub secondary.
- Soft lamp-night atmosphere wash over real desk screenshot.
- One mystical-editorial support sentence under the H1.

### 2. Value Proposition [Score: 5/10]
**Findings:**
- “Chapter is the center” is good but undersells why *this* desk.
- Features listed without lore (Staff / Shelf / Sky / Quiet).
- 4U: Useful yes; Unique partly; Urgent no (and we will not fake urgency); Ultra-specific weak.

**Fixes (Priority: HIGH):**
- Add “The Staff” lore section and denser Desk / Fence sections with concrete mechanisms.

### 3. Social Proof [Score: 4/10]
**Findings:**
- No testimonials (intentional — no invented social proof).
- Stance proof (PD shelf, Isaiah 47 fence, offline audio, local keys) is buried.

**Fixes (Priority: MEDIUM):**
- Surface stance proof as “The Fence” + FAQ instead of fake logos/counts.

### 4. Features and Benefits [Score: 5/10]
**Findings:**
- Triptych links to blogs but blurbs are one-liners.
- Screenshots help; feature→benefit translation is light.

**Fixes (Priority: HIGH):**
- Richer triptych copy; expand blogs to detailed articles as proof depth.

### 5. Objection Handling [Score: 3/10]
**Findings:**
- No FAQ. Astrology/cloud/Bible-version fears unanswered on-page.

**Fixes (Priority: HIGH):**
- FAQ (cloud? fortunes? which Bibles? free? ritual?) + Fence section.

### 6. Call-to-Action [Score: 5/10]
**Findings:**
- Dual CTAs compete; header CTA also GitHub-first.
- Download appears late and soft.

**Fixes (Priority: HIGH):**
- Download primary in header, hero, and close; GitHub as secondary path.

### 7. Footer and Secondary Elements [Score: 6/10]
**Findings:**
- License/fence lines present; weak final CTA and blog links.

**Fixes (Priority: MEDIUM):**
- Footer links to Blog, Releases, GitHub; repeat fence + Apache/NOTICE.

## A/B Test Recommendations
1. If we make **Download for Windows** the primary hero CTA (vs GitHub), then click-through to Releases will increase because App Download visitors seek the installer first.
2. If we lead the H1 with mystical lore (“Lamp-lit desk for the Word”) vs benefit (“Study desk you keep”), then time-on-page will rise among lore-seeking readers because atmosphere matches brand promise.
3. If we place the Fence/FAQ before the final download, then bounce-at-close will fall because astrology/cloud objections are answered before the ask.
