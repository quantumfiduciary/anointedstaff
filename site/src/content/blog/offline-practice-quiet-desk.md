Practice opens on **Temple** first: gates of body, soul, and spirit with verses already in the library, plus a spoken walk baked offline into the build.

## What stays offline

- **Temple guide** and Meditate session intros — River voice, local mp3.
- **Hebrew Hear** on Gematria Learn / letter Cards (22 letters + sofit) and featured Hitchcock names.
- **Quiet music** under Meditate, Scripture Listen, and Extra-canon — soft instrumental that ducks under spoken audio.
- Desk **voice cues** for Drill and Cards.

Regeneration happens outside the running desk (see the repo docs). While you study, there is no live ElevenLabs or Comfy call.

## Five appearances

Light, Dark, Mystic, Parchment, and Ink share the same Staff Gold voltage and the same type stack: Outfit for chrome, Fraunces for titles, Literata for Scripture, Noto for Hebrew and Greek. Pick any look in Settings → Desk or the View menu; the topbar still cycles.

## Privacy posture that matches the audio

API keys default to **session-only**. Leave-device confirms cover place search, web search, and cloud model tests. That matches the Practice story: the quiet parts of the desk should not quietly phone home.
