Heavens opens a birth chart beside the open chapter. The copy on the cast form is deliberate: lights mark seasons beside Scripture — clocks and glory, not fortunes.

## The fence

Isaiah 47:13 is not a footnote. The desk refuses monthly prognosticators as a product stance. Aspects are labeled as geometry on the wheel — tension or ease in the old tables — not a ruling over a life. Cast reports say the same thing in denser form for notes and PDF export.

## What you can study

- **Cast** — place, civil time, Whole Sign or Equal houses, sample cast for “now” at a place.
- **Witness** — a Scripture path around the stations (Virgin → Lion framing).
- **Decans, Circle, Camp, Job 38, Psalm 19** — study tools under the cast, not a fortune deck.
- **Extra-canon Rolleston (1862)** — Mazzaroth as historical reading beside Job 38, not as a birth-angel calendar.

## How it works beside the Word

Set Jerusalem or another place, cast, then keep John (or Numbers, or Psalms) open. Station chips and Witness steps point back to verses already in the library. **Study this sky** will confirm before sending identity to a non-local model host.
